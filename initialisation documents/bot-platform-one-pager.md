# [Working Title]: A Niche AI Bot Platform
*Proposal for [Partner name] · from [Your name] · [Date]*

## The idea
People create an AI bot with its own personality and interests. It researches a topic all day, sends its owner a digest of what it found, and talks to other people's bots. The owner reads the results.

## You had this first, and the market has since backed it
You were working on this when Moltbook launched. Moltbook shipped first, but it proved the demand: within about a week of its January 2026 launch it reportedly had 37,000+ AI agents and over a million people watching, and Meta acquired it in March 2026. The category is real.

What Moltbook *isn't* is a product for ordinary people. You have to run your own agent framework, humans only watch, and there's no reason to come back. That leaves room for a niche, owner-focused version.

## What we'd do differently
- **Niche first.** Launch with guitarists: an audience I can reach directly and whose output quality I can judge myself.
- **Useful to the owner.** A daily digest of what their bot found, not just bots chatting.
- **No setup, sandboxed.** Bots never touch a user's files or accounts.
- **Cost-capped.** Scheduled runs and hard token limits keep cost to roughly £0.15 to £0.50 per active user per month *(my estimate)*.
- **One engine, many niches.** The niche is configuration, so your market can be a second test on the same platform.

## The plan: 10 weeks, about £500 cap
- **Weeks 1 to 3:** concierge test with 20 to 50 guitarists (semi-manual digests). In parallel, 8 to 10 conversations with potential customers in your network.
- **Weeks 4 to 10:** skeletal platform, invite-only, a few hundred users, hard cost ceiling.
- **Decision points:** end of weeks 4 and 10. Continue, pivot or stop.
- **We track:** digest open rate, day-7 return, sharing, willingness to pre-pay, and B2B interest in a paid pilot.

## What I'm asking from you
You don't need to be sold on the whole thing. I'm asking you to be convinced enough to look at a working MVP, and the plan lets you walk away cheaply at each decision point.

1. A small, capped test budget of about **£500** **[confirm amount]**.
2. 5 to 10 introductions to potential B2B, creator or community customers.
3. A second niche from your world to run on the same engine.
4. Two decision meetings (weeks 4 and 10).

## Honest economics
As a purely consumer product this is a modest business. My rough model gives an expected return of around £6k excluding any acquisition, for a lot of effort. **The real bet is the engine.** If it works, the money is likely in B2B or creator use, and your network is what makes that testable. Consumer numbers are proof the engine works, not the end goal.

## Downside and upside
- **Downside:** about £500 plus my time, capped by the decision gates.
- **Upside:** a working engine, real evidence on which market to pursue, and options including a B2B pivot, licensing or a sale.
