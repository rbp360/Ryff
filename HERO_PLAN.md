# HERO_PLAN.md — RYFF Marketing & Landing Page Plan

## 1. Positioning & Value Proposition
**Core Premise:** A first-time visitor must grasp within 10 seconds:
> *"RYFF reads the guitar world for you, knows your gear, and gives you a second opinion."*

- **SongDeck Visual Identity:** Pure black background (`#000`), stark card surfaces (`#121212`), high-contrast SongDeck green (`#22c55e`) for primary calls to action, SongDeck blue (`#38bdf8`) for technical highlights, and bold uppercase geometric headings (Lemon Milk / Montserrat). No gradients, no bubbly cards, zero emoji clutter.

---

## 2. Copy & Hero Section

### Above the Fold (Hero Screen)
- **Kicker:** `SONGDECK GUITAR INTELLIGENCE`
- **Headline (H1):** `YOUR RIG. YOUR NEWS. A BOT WITH AN OPINION.`
- **Sub-headline:** `RYFF ingests the entire guitar world across 40+ outlets, matches stories and used deals directly to what you own and want, and delivers sharp daily takes from two opinionated AI bots.`
- **Secondary Feature Callout (Maintenance & Logging):**
  `+ TWO-TAP GEAR LOGGING: Speak or type maintenance, string health, pickup swaps, and repair logs on every instrument in your rig.`
- **Primary Call to Action (CTA):** `[ GET STARTED FREE ]` (SongDeck green solid button)
- **Secondary Call to Action:** `[ READ TODAY'S DIGEST › ]` (Transparent with hairline border)

---

## 3. How It Works (3-Step Pipeline)

```
[ STEP 1: LOG YOUR RIG ]
Speak or type your guitars, amps, and pedals. Ryff builds your digital rig room,
tracks string age, and records your gear maintenance in seconds.
              ↓
[ STEP 2: RADAR READS THE WORLD ]
Every morning and evening, Ryff ingests 40+ premier guitar feeds, forums, YouTube
channels, and Reverb listings—tagging stories that directly match your gear.
              ↓
[ STEP 3: HANK & VEE CLASH ]
Hank (the vintage luthier) and Vee (the modern modeller) debate the news in public.
You get a sharp, one-line takeaway and can jump in 1-on-1 to get their verdict.
```
